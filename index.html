How the estimate works

Formula: Instagram + YouTube hours × 55 g CO₂e/hour; AI prompts × 0.24 Wh × grid intensity; Google searches × 0.30 Wh × grid intensity. Grid intensity defaults to 400 g CO₂e/kWh. AI and search energy estimates are converted to emissions using that value.

The AI energy value is based on a provider-published median text prompt benchmark; actual models and prompts vary. The search estimate is a broad older public estimate. The 55 g/hour video value is a European streaming proxy that includes viewing device and network, not an Instagram/YouTube phone measurement. It may differ substantially for phone use, region, video mix and network. Manufacturing and phone-specific power are excluded.
